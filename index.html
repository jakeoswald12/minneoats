Meet Allie & Maria

One college apartment. Lines out the door.

In 2017 we started serving hot bowls of oatmeal to friends from our apartment at the University of Minnesota. The line kept growing, so we took it off campus. Today we bake granola in small batches and bring oatmeal bars to events, with the same goal we had on day one: wholesome food and real community.
