Meet Allie & Maria

One college apartment. Lines out the door.

In 2017 we started serving hot bowls of oatmeal to friends from our apartment at the University of Minnesota. The line kept growing, so we took it off campus. Today we bake granola in small batches in Blaine, Minnesota, and bring oatmeal bars to events, with the same goal we had on day one: wholesome food and real community.

      Every box includes Build Your Signature Bowl, plus one more activity that you pick.

      Allie and Maria aren't releasing their recipes publicly. Members will get a few founder-approved oatmeal recipes. Which ones is still being decided.

      Our story

      Two women, one apartment, one very full line.

      Allie and Maria started Minneoats in 2017 with fresh oatmeal and a lot of friends. This is how a college apartment became granola on shelves across Minnesota.

      Our mission

      To share the love of Jesus through wholesome food and meaningful community.

      2017

      The apartment

      Minneoats began as an informal cafe in a University of Minnesota college apartment, serving fresh bowls of oatmeal to friends.

      Soon

      Lines out the door

      Word spread fast and the lines kept growing. People came for the warm bowls and stayed for the company.

      Then

      Oatmeal catering

      Demand grew so we took the bowls and bars to events, and built the favorites our customers still ask about, like Cinneapolis.

      Now

      Granola, baked small

      Today we bake granola in small batches at the TC Farm Kitchen in Blaine, Minnesota, with organic oats, almonds and real maple.

      On shelves near you
